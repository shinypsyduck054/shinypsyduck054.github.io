TRADEDRAFT

VIABILITY SCORE
MARKET · COMPETITION · EXECUTION
6.5/10

Dead-simple AI estimate writer for solo tradespeople. Plain-English job description → professional, itemized estimate PDF in under 2 minutes. No full CRM or platform required. Targets plumbers, electricians, roofers, HVAC techs. $15–19/month. Note: see also RoofDraft — the roofing-specific vertical of this concept. Pitched May 27, 2026.

R&D · SUBSCRIPTION · SOLO TRADE MARKET
THE GAP

Full platforms (Jobber $49+, ServiceTitan $398+, Housecall Pro $65+, Contractor Foreman $49+) all bundle estimates inside full CRMs. Solo tradespeople writing 3–10 estimates/week in Word or by hand have no targeted, affordable AI tool.

Zero search results for a standalone AI estimate/quote writer for solo contractors at sub-$30/month were found. The CRM providers have priced out the solo operator who just wants to look professional on an estimate without buying a $398/month platform.

THE PRODUCT

Tradesperson describes the job in plain English → AI generates a professional, itemized estimate PDF: customer info, job description, line items with labor + materials, taxes, payment terms. Download or email directly to the customer. Under 2 minutes.

Defensible via: Trade-specific pricing templates (plumbing rates, HVAC rates, regional labor costs) + customer history creating switching cost. Distribution: r/Contractor, r/HomeImprovement (890K), Facebook contractor groups. See also RoofDraft for the roofing-specific implementation.

THE VERDICT
R&D · RELATED TO ROOFDRAFT

This is the general category; RoofDraft is the vertical MVP. Building TradeDraft as a multi-trade platform after RoofDraft validates the model is the right sequence. The gap is real, confirmed, and the customer is a small business owner (not in distress) willing to pay $15–19/month for professional-looking estimates.
