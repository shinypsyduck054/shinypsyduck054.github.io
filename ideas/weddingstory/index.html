WEDDINGSTORY

VIABILITY SCORE
MARKET · COMPETITION · EXECUTION
7.0/10

Passive-capture wedding keepsake book. Guests dump phone photos via a per-wedding QR code; the couple's own roll syncs through an app; the venue timeline + vendor list anchor the day. AI stitches it into a 40-60 page hardcover bound book. Couple orders one for themselves and N copies for parents, siblings, grandparents. Same Prodigi pipeline as AirbnbBook + FamilyScript. Vertical under CapsuleStory. Captured June 25, 2026.

R&D · HIGHEST ARPU VERTICAL · GUEST PHOTO AGGREGATION + AI NARRATIVE
THE GAP

Wedding photography costs $3K-12K and produces a curated photographer's gallery. Wedding-book products (Shutterfly, Mixbook, Artifact Uprising) cost $80-300 and require the couple to design every page themselves. Neither captures the wedding from thirty guest phones, where the real moments live. The bride doesn't see the cousin's table laughing during her vows. The mother-in-law's photo of the flower girl asleep at the reception never reaches anyone.

There is no product that aggregates the entire wedding from every angle, organizes by time of day, identifies the moments worth narrating, and ships bound hardcover books to the family. The pieces exist separately. Nobody has stitched them.

THE CAPTURE PROTOCOL
DAY-OF — QR code on table cards / printed insert / venue display. Guests scan and upload from their phones to a per-wedding bucket. Photos arrive with timestamps and EXIF data.
COUPLE'S ROLL — couple installs the app pre-wedding. Their camera roll syncs in the background on the day-of.
PHOTOGRAPHER GALLERY — optional integration with Pixieset, ShootProof, SmugMug for pro photos to mix in.
TIMELINE ANCHORS — venue + vendor schedule (ceremony 4pm, first dance 7:30pm, last call 11pm) supplied by the couple in a 5-min form. AI matches photos to events using timestamps.
AUDIO (optional) — vow recordings, toast recordings if the couple has them, transcribed and woven into the narrative.
THE PRODUCT

40-60 page hardcover wedding book. AI-generated narrative that follows the timeline: getting ready, ceremony, cocktail hour, dinner, dancing, send-off. Photos curated and laid out by the system; AI writes connective text in a warm but plain voice (the couple chooses tone: classic, romantic, irreverent). Couple reviews a digital proof, makes edits, approves. Prodigi prints and ships.

Pricing: $199 for the couple's master copy, $149 per additional copy shipped to parents / grandparents / wedding party. Optional $49 digital PDF to share widely. Typical order: 5-8 copies = $895-1,393 per wedding.

DISTRIBUTION

Wedding planners are the highest-leverage channel — one planner introduces dozens of couples per year. The Knot, Zola, and Joy let vendors list. Wedding photographers are a complementary channel (we are not their competitor; we use their photos as one input among many). Pinterest + TikTok wedding planning content has massive reach for the bride-direct funnel. Buying event lands 1-3 months post-wedding when the photographer's gallery has been seen but the desire for something physical and bound is still fresh.

COMPETITOR SWEEP
  • Shutterfly / Mixbook / Artifact Uprising — DIY photo book templates. Couple does all design work.
  • Mosaic by Wedding Album — premium printing, photographer-fed, not guest-aggregated.
  • Veri / WedShoots / Wedfuly — guest photo collection apps that produce digital albums or slideshows. None ship hardcover books.
  • Pixieset / ShootProof / SmugMug — pro photographer gallery hosts. Not a book product.
  • Postable — thank-you cards only.

The pattern: guest-aggregation tools exist as digital albums. Hardcover book tools exist as DIY. Nobody combines guest aggregation, AI narrative, and shipped hardcover at scale.

THE HARD PARTS — HONEST
  • Photo curation is taste-loaded. Guests upload 200+ photos; the bride wants the 60 that flatter the day. AI can rank by smile + composition + people-in-frame, but the bride's review-and-prune step is non-negotiable. Build for that workflow, do not pretend to skip it.
  • Privacy for guests. Some guests will not consent to a photo of them being printed in someone's book. Default to face-blur-with-opt-in for non-VIPs; let the couple flag VIPs (immediate family, wedding party) as always-in.
  • Tone mismatch is brutal. AI-written copy that reads like a corporate brochure ruins the book. Three tone modes (classic / romantic / irreverent) with strong example text in each, generated samples shown to the couple before they commit.
  • The post-wedding window is small. Honeymoon brain wears off in 2-3 months. Aggressive in-product nudges post-wedding to convert before the couple loses momentum.
THE VERDICT
R&D · HIGHEST ARPU IN THE CAPSULESTORY LINE

The wedding vertical is where average order value clears $1K. Multi-copy buying is the default (parents, grandparents, wedding party). Other CapsuleStory verticals top out at $99-149 per family; weddings clear five to ten times that per wedding.

The competition is fragmented. Guest aggregation, AI narrative, and hardcover printing all exist separately. Nobody has stitched them. The execution risk is curation taste, not novel technology.

Sequence behind SeasonStory. Sports ships first as the cleanest capture profile. Wedding is the second vertical worth shipping under the CapsuleStory stack because the ARPU justifies the curation-tooling investment.
