Passive-capture vacation / family trip keepsake book. Phone camera roll syncs through an app during the trip; geolocation + dates anchor the days; light human input adds a paragraph per stop. AI writes a day-by-day narrative travelogue with photos laid in. Same Prodigi pipeline as AirbnbBook + FamilyScript. Vertical under CapsuleStory. Captured June 25, 2026.

R&D · CROWDED BUT NO PASSIVE-CAPTURE WINNER · HORIZON-TWO VERTICAL
THE GAP

Phone photo apps already cluster vacation photos by date and location (Apple's Memories, Google Photos, Mylio). What they produce is a digital slideshow that the user watches once and forgets. Photo book apps (Chatbooks, Mixbook, Shutterfly) let the user manually build a book from those same photos but require hours of selection, layout, and caption-writing per trip.

The middle ground does not exist: passive capture from the camera roll, AI-written day-by-day narrative, hardcover book shipped without the user having to design or compose anything. The trip ends, a notification fires, the user taps approve, the book ships.

THE CAPTURE PROTOCOL
CAMERA ROLL SYNC — app installed pre-trip. Photos uploaded automatically while on wifi. Geolocation + timestamps come from EXIF.
DAY ANCHORS — AI clusters photos by day and location automatically. Light human input: name each day a phrase ("Day 3 — Sintra"), add one or two sentences if desired.
VOICE MEMOS (optional) — quick voice notes from the trip get transcribed and woven in as first-person color.
CONTEXT FETCH — for each cluster location, AI pulls a paragraph of historical / local color (which town, what it is known for) to ground the narrative.
RECEIPTS (optional) — forwarded email receipts (flights, hotels, restaurants) add anchor data without manual entry.
THE PRODUCT

30-50 page hardcover travel book. AI writes day-by-day narrative in a warm first-person voice. Photos curated and laid out; user reviews and approves the proof. Prodigi prints and ships.

Pricing: $99-129 per book. Family trips typically order 2-4 copies (one per household member who wants one), so ~$200-516 per trip. Optional $29 digital PDF.

COMPETITOR SWEEP
  • Apple Memories / Google Photos — auto-clustering by trip, digital slideshow output. Free. Not a book.
  • Chatbooks — auto-prints monthly photo books from Instagram / camera roll. Square, paperback, single-month windows. Not narrative.
  • Mixbook / Shutterfly / Artifact Uprising / Mpix — DIY photo books. Hours of design work per book.
  • Polarsteps — trip tracking app with optional photo book purchase. Book is map + photos with auto-captioned dates; not a written narrative.
  • Mosaic by Plum — minimum-touch photo book from camera roll, square 7x7 format. No narrative; book is a layout.

The crowded part is photo-clustering and photo-book layout. The empty part is AI-written travelogue narrative that reads like a memoir. Polarsteps is the closest in spirit but stops at maps + captioned dates.

THE HARD PARTS — HONEST
  • Bigger fish swim here. Apple and Google could ship this. They probably won't because the bound-book exit doesn't fit their business model, but the input layer is already commoditized in their apps.
  • Narrative voice across cultures is loaded. "Charming little town" tone reads colonial in some contexts. Tone modes (factual / personal / warm) and good defaults matter.
  • Ranking against Chatbooks is hard. Chatbooks has 1M+ users and ships paperbacks monthly for $10. The differentiator has to be narrative depth + hardcover keepsake quality, not price.
  • Single-trip purchase has low LTV. Unlike family or sports where multiple seasons / years create repeat buyers, a vacation is often a one-off. Build for the high-value trips (honeymoon, big family trip, milestone) not the weekend getaway.
THE VERDICT
R&D · HORIZON-TWO VERTICAL · DO NOT SHIP THIRD

The pattern fits, the differentiation is thin. Same Prodigi pipeline, same passive-capture approach. But Chatbooks already eats the low-end and Apple/Google already cluster the photos. Winning requires a clearly better narrative output than anyone else has shipped, which is the part most likely to fall short of expectations early.

Save it for vertical four or five. SeasonStory ships first. WeddingStory ships second. D&D ships third as a partner integration. Vacation comes after the AI narrative composer has been tuned across three real verticals and the bound-book quality bar is set. Shipping vacation before the composer is good produces a generic-feeling product that hurts the whole CapsuleStory line.
