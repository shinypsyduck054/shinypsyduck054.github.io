THE GAP

Reconsideration of Value (ROV) submissions live or die on the quality of the alternative comps the consumer attaches. The appraiser chose comps that justified a low number. To win an ROV, the buyer or seller has to surface comps the appraiser missed or chose poorly, with proper per-sqft adjustment math.

Consumers can't do this well on their own. They don't know USPAP comp-selection rules, don't have $400-700 for a second appraisal, and their agent helps inconsistently. Dwellworks data: only ~24% of ROVs result in a modified appraisal, mostly because the submitted evidence is weak.

The 2024 Interagency Guidance on ROV (Federal Reserve, CFPB, OCC, FDIC, NCUA, finalized July 2024) plus the 2024 PAVE Action Plan now legally require lenders to disclose ROV rights to borrowers. The federal regulatory floor is creating the market. Consumers being told "you have the right to challenge" but given no tool to do it well.

Pairs with AppraisalFight as the premium tier in a two-SKU funnel: AppraisalFight for buyers/sellers who want a guide and templated letter ($29 DIY), Comp Defender for ones who want the AI to do the actual analytical comp work ($99-149 done-for-you).

THE PRODUCT
  • Address + appraiser's comps in: User enters subject property, transaction price, and the comps the appraiser chose (from the appraisal PDF)
  • AI comp research: Scans public records, MLS-syndicated portals, and county assessor data for stronger comps within 6 months and the right sqft/bed/bath band
  • Adjustment math: Per-sqft analysis, dollar adjustments for differences (sqft, beds, baths, garage, lot, condition) — the methodology a licensed appraiser would use
  • ROV-ready packet: Formatted exactly for lender intake. PDF the consumer attaches to their own ROV submission
  • Defensibility brief: Short narrative explaining why the appraiser's comps were inappropriate (too distant, wrong era, wrong condition tier) and why the new comps are stronger
  • $99-149 one-time. User does NOT pay if they don't have an active ROV opportunity within 30 days of purchase
COMPETITOR SWEEP

Kukun (mykukun.com) is the closest adjacent threat. $5M ARR, $15M valuation. Free consumer AVM (KAVM) with comps, adjustments, PICO property condition score, complexity score. Just published "How to Influence AI Home Appraisals 2026: The AVM Defense" — they are content-marketing INTO this positioning. They have NOT productized an ROV-submission-packet specifically. The Sword of Damocles is real: they could ship a paid ROV upsell in 6-12 months and crush a paid entrant by bundling it on their existing free platform.

HouseCanary sells ComeHome to lenders and brokers (B2B). No direct-to-consumer ROV product.

HomeLight has a blog post explaining ROVs, then refers consumers to a real estate agent. Monetizes via agent referrals, not via product.

B2B/enterprise appraisal-review players (Reggora, Class Valuation, Clear Capital, ServiceLink) all sell to lenders. None target borrowers.

RPR + CloudCMA (the agent-side CMA tools we vetted while killing CMAReady) are gated behind a real estate license. FSBO buyers/sellers without an agent cannot use them.

Brand space clear: CompDefender, RebutValue, ROVPro, AppraiserShield, AppraisalAppeal — none of these names are taken by an active SaaS product.

THE VERDICT
R&D · NARROW WEDGE, REAL TAILWIND, 12-MONTH WINDOW

Regulatory tailwind is the credibility engine. 2024 Interagency Guidance + PAVE Action Plan + FHFA/HUD/VA rules formally require lenders to disclose ROV rights. The market is being legally created right now.

Not a kit shape. Primary deliverable is analytical comp evidence (data product), not paperwork. Consumer writes their own ROV submission and attaches our comp packet as evidence. Survives the 2026-06-23 kit ban as a different structural category.

Customer state OK. Buyer or seller in a $20-50k appraisal gap is a voluntary commercial actor with clear ROI ($99-149 to save $20k+), not someone in housing/medical/financial distress. Cleaner ETHICS posture than CMAReady was. Honest messaging required: "we maximize your odds, we don't guarantee" — never sell certainty on a process with a ~24% accept rate.

Sword of Damocles: Kukun. $5M ARR free incumbent with adjacent positioning, no productized ROV packet yet. If they ship one, we get squeezed. The race is real, the 12-month window is real, the wedge (ROV-submission-formatted packaging vs raw AVM data) is real but narrow.
