THE GAP

All search results for 504 AI tools are B2B teacher-side plan generators (OpenEduCat, musely.ai, flintk12.com, TeacherToolAI, PlanBuddy.ai, edugenius.app) — they help teachers draft accommodation plans AFTER a student is already identified. Zero parent-facing 504 evaluation REQUEST letter generators were found.

iepadvocate.ai covers IEP special education evaluations — distinct from 504 procedural requests. The critical legal hook: a written request for 504 evaluation triggers mandatory school response obligations under Section 504 and ADA Title II. Without a formal written request, schools routinely delay indefinitely. This gap is not about getting accommodations — it's about forcing the school to evaluate in the first place.

Parents with ADHD kids in r/ADHD (1.1M) post constantly asking "how do I get my kid a 504?" with no dedicated tool. Special ed advocates charge $150–300/hour. Direct pattern match to AccommoDoc (workplace ADA accommodation kit) which validated the format.

THE PRODUCT
  • 504 evaluation request letter: Formally triggers school's legal obligation to evaluate under Section 504 and ADA Title II

  • Accommodation ideas checklist: Common accommodations for ADHD, dyslexia, anxiety, chronic illness, mobility issues
  • Denial appeal template: What to do if school denies the evaluation request (full kit only)
