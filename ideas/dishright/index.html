THE GAP

The FCC OTARD rule (47 CFR Part 1, Subpart S, Section 1.4000) has been in place since 1996 and has been repeatedly upheld and expanded. It prohibits any restriction that impairs the installation, maintenance, or use of an antenna or satellite dish under 1 meter. HOAs are explicitly named. The rule covers DirecTV dishes, DISH Network dishes, Starlink dishes, and outdoor TV antennas.

Despite being federal law for nearly 30 years, HOAs routinely send cease-and-desist letters, levy fines, and threaten legal action against homeowners with satellite dishes. Tens of millions of DirecTV and DISH subscribers are in HOA communities. Starlink adoption is accelerating fast. Every one of them who gets an HOA letter is a potential customer.

No AI tool writes the OTARD demand letter. Dish providers (DirecTV, DISH) give customers a generic FAQ but no actual letter to send. The homeowner either folds under HOA pressure or spends hours researching federal regulations they don't understand. Both outcomes are terrible when the right answer is a single well-cited letter.
