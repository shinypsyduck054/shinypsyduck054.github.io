THE GAP

Google Ads suspends accounts via opaque automated systems. The notification gives a policy code with no plain-English explanation. The advertiser has one shot at an appeal — botched first attempts trigger longer review windows or permanent disable. There is no specialized self-serve tool. Either you DIY through Google's 5-step community guide (most appeals are denied) or you hire a Google Ads suspension agency at $300-800+ per recovery. The long tail of SMB advertisers can't justify the agency price for a single recovery.

YouTube videos with confirmed views show advertisers manually pasting their suspension notice into Gemini and asking for an appeal letter. Demand is real. No product serves it.

THE PRODUCT

SMB advertiser enters their Google Ads suspension reason code + business description → instant $29 kit:

  • Plain-English policy decoder — what they actually violated and why Google flagged it
  • Structured appeal response addressing each Google policy point by code
  • Documentation checklist — business license, website screenshots, billing verification, identity docs
  • Escalation path if denied — Google Ads Partner escalation, Support ticket sequence, BBB complaint tactic for triggering human review

$29 one-time. No subscription. Output delivered in under 2 minutes. User copy-pastes the appeal into Google's form (no auto-send — keeps us out of the AI Agent Policy lane).
