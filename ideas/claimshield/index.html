CLAIMSHIELD

AI dispute response generator for marketplace sellers fighting fraudulent buyer claims on Amazon, eBay, and Etsy. $19 one-time. Sellers currently pay $100–300 Upwork specialists per response. No dedicated product exists.

R&D FAVORITE · GREENLIT · HIGH CONFIDENCE GAP
THE GAP

Amazon's A-to-Z Guarantee and eBay's Money Back Guarantee both heavily favor buyers. A buyer can claim "item not received" or "not as described" — even fraudulently — and the seller gets 48–72 hours to submit a structured written response or the claim is automatically decided against them.

Most small sellers don't know what Amazon's claim reviewers actually look for. They write emotional, incomplete responses and lose claims they should win. The result is a double hit: the refund is lost and the seller takes an Order Defect Rate (ODR) account health strike. Enough strikes = account suspension.

~25% of buyers who request refunds plan to keep the product. ~15% of returns are fraudulent. Sellers currently pay $100–300 Upwork specialists per response — or they wing it. No purpose-built tool exists for this.

THE PRODUCT

Seller enters: platform (Amazon/eBay/Etsy), claim type (A-to-Z / Money Back / Purchase Protection), claim details, their evidence (tracking, messages, photos). AI generates a formatted, policy-cited dispute response letter in the exact structure each platform's claim reviewers evaluate.

Price: $19 one-time per response. Optional $39 bundle (up to 3 claim responses). Clear ROI: if the claim is $50+, $19 to fight it properly is an obvious bet.

The real value beyond the refund: a won claim = no ODR defect. Protecting account health has compounding value for any active seller. Even a $30 claim is worth fighting properly for this reason.
