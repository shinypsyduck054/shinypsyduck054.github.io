THE GAP

Every craft brewery, winery, meadery, and cidery in the US must obtain a Certificate of Label Approval (COLA) from the federal TTB (Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau) before selling a single bottle. The TTB has mandatory label elements -- brand name, class/type designation, net contents, alcohol content, country of origin, health warning statement, responsible advertiser -- and strict rules about font size, prohibited claims, and wording. Rejections are common and delay product launches by weeks.

The problem: most small craft producers learn TTB requirements through trial and error, expensive consultants, or dense federal PDFs. TTB compliance consultants charge $300+/hour. The regs themselves are public but buried in the Code of Federal Regulations (27 CFR Parts 4, 5, 7). No AI tool pre-checks labels before submission.

THE PRODUCT

Producer enters beverage type (beer, wine, spirits, cider, mead), ABV, brand name, origin, net contents, and intended claims -- AI cross-references against public TTB regulations for mandatory elements, prohibited claims, health warning requirements, and font rules -- outputs a pre-submission compliance report with pass/fail on each element and specific fix instructions for any failures.
