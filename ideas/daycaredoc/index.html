THE GAP

Every state requires home daycare providers to submit 10+ written policy documents as part of their licensing application — sick child policies, emergency procedures, discipline policies, medication policies, and more. These must be written professionally and match state-specific regulatory language.

New providers face the same choice: spend 10+ hours researching and writing policies from scratch using state government PDFs as a guide, or pay a childcare licensing consultant $200–500 to walk them through it. There is no tool in between.

California's sick policy requirements are not Texas's. A generic Word template from a nonprofit website fails both. The state-specificity is exactly where AI adds irreplaceable value — and where existing solutions fall apart.

THE PRODUCT

Provider enters: state, daycare capacity, ages served, and a few setup preferences. Claude generates a complete policy kit — 10–15 state-compliant documents — formatted for licensing submission. $49 one-time. Instant PDF delivery.

  • State-specific language — matches the regulatory terminology your state licensing office expects
  • Complete policy set — sick child, emergency, medication, discipline, transportation, nutrition, and more
  • Personalized — your capacity, ages served, and setup built into every document
  • Instant PDF — ready to attach to your licensing application the same day
