THE GAP

Dized — the only real competitor — requires publisher partnership for every game it covers. That caps them at ~200-400 titles. BoardGameGeek lists 170,000+ games. For the other 169,600+ games, hobbyists have no dedicated teach tool.

THE PRODUCT

Enter a game name (or upload the rulebook PDF). Select player count and experience level (first-timers vs. veterans who just need a refresher). Get a structured teach script: objective first, core actions next, round structure, then scoring — in the order that makes each rule land before the next one depends on it. Talking points included. What to hold back until it comes up naturally in play, flagged.

Price: $29 one-time per game, or a free tier (1 game) to reduce friction. No subscription. No churn.

Distribution: r/boardgames (4.5M members), r/tabletopgames, BGG forums, BoardGameGeek "Tools & Resources" category. Post with a Wingspan screenshot. First sale target: within 2 weeks of launch.

WHY NO PUBLISHER LICENSES ARE NEEDED

Game mechanics and rules are not copyrightable — Baker v. Selden (1879) established that systems and processes cannot be owned, only specific expression. TeachIt generates original teaching scripts; it does not reproduce rulebook text. The output is new, original content informed by an understanding of the game mechanics.

For the PDF upload flow: users provide their own copy of a rulebook they already own — the same as uploading a PDF to NotebookLM. The output is transformative (a new teach script, not a reproduction). This is legally equivalent to what Google's NotebookLM does, and no publisher has challenged that behavior.

Game names can be referenced for identification (nominative fair use — the same way you can say "this works with Wingspan" without needing Stonemaier's permission). No publisher logos, no box art, no implied endorsement.

Why Dized needed licenses and TeachIt doesn't: Dized reproduces publisher-approved game content verbatim with publisher branding — that's a derivative work. TeachIt's output is original content generated by the tool. Completely different legal category.
