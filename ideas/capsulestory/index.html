R&D · PLATFORM PATTERN · SHARED PRODIGI STACK · VERTICALS REUSE 70% OF CODE
THE PATTERN

Every keepsake-worthy life chapter has the same three ingredients: a window of time, a stream of passive media, and a moment afterward when someone wants it bound up and shipped. Wedding photos. End-of-season team highlights. The campaign your D&D group played for two years. The kid's first year. The week the family roadtripped.

All of them have the same input shape (photos with timestamps, audio with speakers, scores or milestones, plus a few human notes), the same AI synthesis stage (narrative composer plus optional illustration), and the same fulfillment exit (Prodigi prints the hardcover and ships to N families). The verticals differ only in capture protocol and tone of voice.

THE STACK
CAPTURE — photos + timestamps + light human input (form, voice memo, score). Vertical-specific intake (sports parents upload photos all season, D&D table records sessions, wedding guests dump shots from one day).
SYNTHESIS — shared LLM composer that takes structured intake and writes a 20-40 page narrative. Optional AI illustration layer (scenes, not portraits).
FULFILLMENT — Prodigi API. Same pipeline already greenlit for AirbnbBook and FamilyScript. One-off hardcover, optional PDF, ships direct to family addresses.
BUYING EVENT — one organizer (coach, DM, bride, dad) creates the project. N families pay $99-149 each for their own copy. School-picture-day economics.
VERTICAL ROADMAP
CAPSULESTORY: SPORTS — youth sports team season book. Easiest capture (photo timestamps + game scores). Distribution via TeamSnap, MaxPreps, coach Facebook groups. Spring + fall buying spikes. Ships first.
CAPSULESTORY: D&D — campaign session book with AI scene art. Capture layer already commoditized (Archivist AI, SessionKeeper, CharGen, Tabletop Arc). Integrate as their output partner; do not compete on recording. Ships second.
CAPSULESTORY: WEDDINGS — guest photo dump + day-of timeline = bound wedding book per family. Highest ARPU. Existing wedding-book market is DIY-only.
CAPSULESTORY: VACATION — family roadtrip or trip-of-a-lifetime, photo dump + geolocation + dates.
CAPSULESTORY: BABY'S YEAR — first year photos + monthly milestones. Recurring annual buyer.
CAPSULESTORY: SENIOR YEAR — high school senior's year captured by parents, gift at graduation.
CAPSULESTORY: REUNION — class reunion or family reunion, multi-perspective same-day capture.

Ship one vertical, validate the rails, fork the front-end for vertical two. Two verticals justifies real abstraction. Eight imagined verticals does not.

COMPETITOR SWEEP

For each vertical, two layers to check: the capture rails and the keepsake artifact.

  • D&D capture: SATURATED. Archivist AI, SessionKeeper, CharGen, Tabletop Arc, RoleForge, The DM's ARK all do session-recording + AI recap + campaign wiki. Some include AI portraits.
  • D&D printed keepsake: EMPTY. None of the above ship a hardcover bound novel to the players.
  • Sports capture: PARTIAL. Waldo Photos does AI-sorted parent photo uploads for athletic departments. Imagen-AI does sports volume photo editing. TeamSnap has photo sharing.
  • Sports printed keepsake: EMPTY. Shutterfly has DIY sports templates. Jostens does school yearbooks (not team-specific). No purpose-built AI sports team season book.
  • Wedding capture: PARTIAL. Many wedding photo apps; few do passive guest aggregation with AI synthesis.
  • Wedding printed keepsake: PARTIAL. Big DIY wedding book market (Shutterfly, Mixbook) but no ambient-capture + AI-synthesized version.

The pattern: capture rails are getting built per-vertical by separate companies. The keepsake-output layer (bound hardcover, shipped to N families) is wide open in every vertical.

ACTIVE vs PASSIVE — WHAT'S NEW HERE

The Prodigi + AI keepsake book pipeline is not new in this vault. There is already a cluster of active-authored products: the user fills a form, pastes notes, or answers a questionnaire, and a hardcover ships out. CapsuleStory's actual novelty is the passive-capture input layer. The user opens the app and forgets it. The system listens or collects in the background. The book emerges without anyone authoring it.

That input shift changes who the buyer is. Active-authored sells to the person willing to do the writing. Passive-captured sells to the busy organizer (coach, DM, bride, dad) who would never sit down to compose a thirty-page narrative but will absolutely tap a button at the end of the season.

THE HARD PARTS — HONEST
  • Audio diarization is hard. Distinguishing five players' table talk from their character voices in a noisy D&D room with snacks and music is real engineering. Whisper-large + pyannote get to roughly 80%; the last 20% is brutal. Sports vertical avoids this entirely.
  • Recording consent. All-friends D&D table is trivial. Sports games are fine for own-team parents; opposing-team kids in frame is a content moderation problem.
  • AI art is socially loaded. D&D players are protective of character portraits commissioned elsewhere. Default to "we generate scene art, not character portraits" until norms are clear.
  • Premature abstraction kills speed. Build SeasonStory standalone with the common stack factored cleanly enough that vertical two shares 70%. Do not build a "platform for ten verticals" before you have two real ones.
THE VERDICT
R&D · PLATFORM PATTERN · SEASONSTORY SHIPS FIRST

The capture layer is saturated, the keepsake artifact layer is empty. Every vertical has the same structure: someone built the photo or audio pipes, nobody built the hardcover book that comes out the other end and ships to twenty families.

The Prodigi pipeline is already greenlit. AirbnbBook and FamilyScript pay for the back-end. Each new CapsuleStory vertical is essentially a new front-end on a pipeline that already works.

Sequence: SeasonStory v1, then D&D as output partner to the existing recording tools, then weddings. Do not preemptively build verticals three through seven. Two shipped verticals earn the right to abstract.
