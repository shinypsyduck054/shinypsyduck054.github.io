THE GAP

Competitive intelligence tools exist in two tiers: enterprise (Crayon, Klue, Kompyte — $25k+/yr, built for SaaS companies tracking press releases and product updates) and nothing. The million-dollar roofing company in Columbus, Ohio has no way to know what its 5 local competitors are charging, what their recent reviews say, or whether they just hired 3 new crews.

HomeServiceBase.com provides national market research statistics — industry-level data, not local competitor tracking. BrightLocal and Semrush track YOUR OWN local SEO, not competitors. Google Alerts is unstructured noise. No product delivers "here's what the 5 companies competing with you right now did this week" at an SMB price point.

The gap is hyperlocal + specific-competitor + actionable synthesis + SMB price. All four together: unoccupied.

THE PRODUCT

Customer onboards once: enters their business name, location, and up to 10 competitor names. Every Monday at 7am, a one-page briefing lands in their inbox. No dashboards to log into. No settings to fiddle with. Just intelligence, delivered.

Sample output: "ABC Roofing dropped their price on gutter installs this week — two new reviews mention 'same day service' as a differentiator. They posted a job for a crew lead, signaling expansion. Recommended: address response time in your own review requests this week."

DATA SOURCES
Google Maps reviews — new reviews + rating delta + owner response analysis
Google Ads Transparency — official public API, what ads competitors are running
Meta Ad Library — official Facebook API (free), Facebook/Instagram ads
Indeed / ZipRecruiter — job postings reveal growth signals (new crew, office staff, sales hires)
Website monitoring — pricing page changes, new service area additions, promotions
THE SCRAPING ARCHITECTURE
SAT 8PM → Report generation jobs queued for all customers due Monday
SAT 9PM → Scrape jobs begin — 1 request per competitor per source, 3-5 min delay between each
             10 competitors × 4 sources = 40 requests, staggered over 20+ hours. Looks organic.
SUN 7PM → All raw data collected and stored in DB
SUN 8PM → Claude synthesis runs (parallel across all customers, fast)
MON 7AM → Email fires. Customer reads it with their morning coffee.

Slow drip = undetectable. No rate limit triggers. The 24-hour window is the protection.

Phase 1 (0–100 customers): Playwright + residential proxy rotation. Phase 2 (100–1,000): SerpAPI for Google Maps, official APIs for Meta/Google Ads, Apify actors for job sites. Phase 3 (1,000+): DataForSEO or BrightData bulk contracts — 80% cost reduction at scale.

HONEST RISKS
  • Data quality: Competitors with minimal online presence generate thin reports. Mitigate with a "low data" fallback message and focus on digitally-active markets first (suburban/metro, not rural).
  • Google Maps rate limiting: Solved by Phase 2 SerpAPI/Outscraper migration — they handle the proxy infrastructure. Do not rely on direct scraping at scale.
  • Report fatigue: If insights aren't consistently "wow," churn spikes at 90 days. Mitigate with an AI quality gate before sending — only surface genuinely notable changes, not noise.
  • Home service CAC: These owners aren't browsing Product Hunt. Distribution requires SEO + Facebook/Instagram ads targeting the vertical + direct outreach to contractor associations.
