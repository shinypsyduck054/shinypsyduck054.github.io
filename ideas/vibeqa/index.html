THE GAP

QA tools exist in two tiers: enterprise and nothing. QA Wolf ($200+/month) is human-assisted and enterprise-only. Momentic serves Notion and Webflow. Mabl has enterprise custom pricing. Checkly ($24/month) requires writing Playwright scripts yourself — that's the product you're trying to avoid needing.

Zero self-serve, zero-config AI flow-discovery product exists at the indie/vibe-coder tier. The gap is: deploy hook → AI does the discovery, AI writes the tests, AI runs the tests, AI alerts you. No human test-writing anywhere in the loop.

Timing is the unlock. Vibe coding is peak-trend in 2026. Cursor and Claude Discord communities are massive and vocal. The customer who ships an app with Cursor on Friday and wants to know if it still works on Monday — that person exists in huge numbers and has no tool.

THE PRODUCT

Connect your deploy hook (Vercel/Netlify/Railway one-click) → VibeQA crawls your app on first connection, discovers user flows (login, signup, checkout, key actions), generates Playwright scripts, and runs them against every new deploy. Failures become Slack/email alerts with a screenshot of exactly what broke.

Pricing: Free tier (1 app, 5 flows). Pro $39/month (5 apps, unlimited flows). Team $99/month (unlimited). First dollar path: Cursor Discord, r/vibecoding, r/SideProject, HN Show HN.

COMPETITOR SWEEP
  • QA Wolf — $200+/month, human-assisted QA engineers write the tests. Enterprise only.
  • Momentic — AI testing, but targets Notion/Webflow/enterprise clients. No self-serve indie tier.
  • Mabl — enterprise custom pricing, no transparent self-serve.
  • Checkly — $24/month but you still write Playwright scripts yourself. Not zero-config.
  • Playwright/Cypress — DIY frameworks. Not a product, requires developer time to set up and maintain.

Gap confirmed: Zero self-serve zero-config AI flow-discovery + auto-test product exists at the indie/vibe-coder price tier.

HONEST RISKS
  • Timing risk: This window is open because vibe coding is new. In 18 months, Vercel or Netlify could bundle basic AI testing. Move fast.
  • AI accuracy: AI-discovered flows will miss edge cases. Set expectations correctly — this catches obvious regressions, not subtle logic bugs.
  • Auth complexity: Authenticated flows require storing test credentials. Security-sensitive. Phase 1 can focus on public flows only.
  • Cost per run: Playwright + Claude synthesis per deploy could add up at scale. Need to price for it.
THE VERDICT
R&D · TIMING-SENSITIVE

The gap is real. Every enterprise QA tool requires a human to write tests. Vibe-coders don't want to write tests — they want to ship. Zero-config AI flow discovery is the unlock that makes QA accessible to this market.

The timing is right. Cursor/Claude Discord communities are massive in 2026. This is the moment to capture this customer before Vercel/Netlify bundle it.

The build is achievable. Playwright is open source, Claude API handles flow synthesis. Deploy hooks are standard. The hard part is AI test reliability — start with simple flows and iterate.
