THE GAP

Generic AI contract reviewers exist (junia.ai, sueletter.com) but are not creator-specific and don't generate FTC disclosure scripts. No dedicated "brand deal contract review + FTC compliance script generator" was found on ProductHunt, IndieHackers, or Reddit.

The FTC compliance angle is the sharpest hook. Every creator knows they must disclose — but very few know the exact platform-specific required language. Getting it wrong means up to $51,744 per violation. Entertainment lawyers charge $300–500/hr for the same review. A $19 kit is a no-brainer for a creator earning $500+ per brand deal.

THE PRODUCT

Creator pastes their brand deal contract text → AI produces a 4-part kit:

  • Plain-English summary: Exclusivity scope, deliverables, kill fee, usage rights, payment schedule
  • Red flag alerts: Perpetual content licenses, missing kill fee, overbroad exclusivity, unclear deliverable specs
  • FTC disclosure scripts: IG caption language, TikTok script, YouTube card + description language, podcast verbal disclosure
  • Negotiation email: Pre-written email for the flagged unfair terms

Pricing: $19/deal one-time. $29/month unlimited. Distribution: r/NewTubers (150K+), r/Twitch (120K+), r/YouTube, creator Discord servers.
