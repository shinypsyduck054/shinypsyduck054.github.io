Coastal Boat Works

Justin's brother Jeremy's landlocked one-person refit shop in Walterboro, SC. Overbooked, underpriced, sitting on a CNC machine and a real craft moat, and currently grinding the owner down. The one-paragraph synthesis

The shop is a real, well-run craft business that's pricing itself like 2014 in a 2026 coastal market, run by Jeremy. The Landscape says “raise prices, niche into EVA decking, fix your web, productize on the CNC.” Why Not says “the boring upside of just doing that is already huge, solo, in Walterboro, in your own time.” The Edge says “if a software + AI team handled everything that isn't craft, the same hours throw off +$490k over five years.” The Partnership says “for a stranger's shop we wouldn't take equity; we'd build Solo Craft Shop OS and they'd be customer-zero.” The Brotherhood says “since it's family with capital and 1.25 hours away, start with Track A relief on the shop and Track B capital into SCSO on a real SAFE.” The Corps says “or, if Jeremy is into the CCC Boys ethos, do it as a collective: a smart-contract ledger that splits surplus across idea, implementation, and infrastructure equally, with Jeremy as the first vertical member or as Justin's co-founder. Same outcome, cleaner family optics, scales beyond him, creates real apprentice jobs in the Lowcountry.” And The Magnolia Wildcard says “there's a third cousin, Joey, who already runs a healthy roofing business and is pushing into Jeremy's territory; the right move with him is none of them as a partnership and all of them as a corps, sequenced, with the Joey conversation happening from a position of strength after the corps is real.” That's the whole project in one breath.
