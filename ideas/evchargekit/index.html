THE GAP

14+ states have enacted "Right to Charge" laws that explicitly prohibit HOAs from banning EV charging station installation. In most states, HOAs can only impose reasonable aesthetic and safety requirements -- they cannot outright deny an EV owner the right to install a Level 2 charger. Yet EV forums, r/electricvehicles (1.5M members), r/teslamotors (900K+), and r/HOA are full of posts from owners who gave up when their HOA said no.

The statutory rights are unambiguous. The awareness is not. ChargePoint, Tesla, and EV charger installers are focused on the technical install -- not on HOA conflicts. The homeowner whose HOA just denied their charger request is entirely on their own.

No AI tool writes the state-specific right-to-charge demand letter. Charger companies don't provide it. The homeowner's only option is hire an attorney (overkill for a clear statutory violation) or give up.
