ROOFDRAFT

AI-powered roofing estimate generator for solo contractors. 8 inputs → professional itemized PDF in under 2 minutes. v1 of a multi-vertical trade estimator platform. Pitched May 27, 2026. Beta tester: confirmed.

R&D · DEEP SCOPING · BETA TESTER LINED UP
THE PITCH

Solo roofers write 3–10 estimates per week in Word, by hand, or in their head. The full platforms — Jobber ($49+/mo), ServiceTitan ($398+/mo), Housecall Pro ($65+/mo) — bundle estimates inside heavy CRMs built for large operations. No standalone, affordable, AI-powered estimate tool exists for the solo or small-crew roofer.

RoofDraft takes 8 inputs and generates a complete, professional, itemized estimate PDF in under 2 minutes. No CRM. No onboarding marathon. No features the contractor doesn't need. The simplest way to create a roofing estimate that exists.

v1 is roofing. The same engine powers PlumbDraft, WireDraft, AirDraft (HVAC), and BuildDraft — each a separate brand, separate SEO footprint, same codebase underneath.

THE 8 INPUTS
1. Customer name + job address
2. Roof size — squares (100 sq ft each), or enter sq ft and we convert
3. Pitch — flat / low / medium / steep (affects material qty + labor rate)
4. Stories — 1 / 2 / 3+ (affects safety setup + labor cost)
5. Tear-off layers — 0 (overlay) / 1 / 2+ layers to remove
6. Shingle tier — Good / Better / Best with price anchors preset
7. Add-ons — checkboxes: ice & water shield, ridge vent, pipe boots (×qty), chimney flashing
8. Notes — anything unusual; voice input supported

AI generates the full itemized estimate from those 8 inputs. Contractor adjusts any line before generating the PDF.

WHAT THE ESTIMATE INCLUDES
  • Tear-off labor (per square × layers)
  • New shingles (per square + 12% waste factor)
  • Underlayment / synthetic felt
  • Ice & water shield (valleys, eaves, penetrations)
  • Starter strips + ridge cap shingles
  • Drip edge + step flashing
  • Pipe boot replacements (per penetration)
  • Ridge vent (linear feet)
  • Chimney / skylight flashing (if flagged)
  • Dumpster + debris disposal
  • Contractor overhead + margin (set in account preferences)
  • Payment terms + labor warranty language
ONBOARDING — 3 FIELDS, DONE
  • Business name + logo upload
  • License number
  • Local labor rate per square (or select regional default)

First estimate in under 5 minutes. That's the promise.

OPEN QUESTIONS FOR R&D

  • PDF output: custom branded template with logo, or contractor uploads their own?
  • Mobile-first or web-first? Roofers are on job sites — does this need to work offline?
  • Distribution: r/Roofing cold outreach, or Facebook contractor groups first?
  • Pricing: flat $19/mo, or per-estimate for very low-volume contractors?
