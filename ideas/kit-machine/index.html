THE GAP

Millions of people search every month for state-specific, situation-specific document templates: habitability demand letters, HOA appeal letters, IRS notice response kits, executor notification letters, ADA accommodation requests. The existing solutions are: (1) generic free templates that don't cite state law, (2) law firms charging $200–500/hour, (3) nothing.

The Spanish, Portuguese, and French versions of these searches are almost completely unoccupied. A search for "carta de reclamación de reparaciones para inquilinos Texas" returns thin blog posts and zero purpose-built tools. The same is true across 200+ niche document categories in these languages. First-mover ranks in weeks, not months, because there is no competition to displace.

THE PRODUCT

Each "kit" is the same formula:
→ Customer lands on a targeted SEO page (e.g., "Texas tenant repair demand letter")
→ Fills a short form (state, situation, their details)
→ Claude generates a state-law-cited, properly-phrased document
→ Stripe checkout ($19–$49 one-time)
→ PDF delivered instantly via email

No subscription. No SaaS complexity. No ongoing support burden. A customer who paid $29 for a PDF three years ago still shows up in the ARR calculation as a repeat visitor if they buy again.

Each EN kit gets a simultaneous ES+PT launch. FR and DE roll in behind. The entire content pipeline is templatized — the marginal cost of each additional language variant is 30–45 minutes of AI-assisted localization, not a rebuild.

COMPETITOR SWEEP

Across 20+ document kit pitches vetted in this vault, the same pattern held: free templates exist but don't cite state law; law firms exist but cost 10–50× more; AI generic letter generators exist but aren't purpose-built for the specific situation; DoNotPay touched many of these verticals but is effectively dead. The niche-specific, state-law-cited, one-time-purchase AI kit is consistently unoccupied. No aggregator has bundled 200+ of these under one brand. No Spanish-first version of this business exists anywhere.

THE INFRASTRUCTURE
PDF PIPELINE: Form → Claude API → formatted PDF → Stripe checkout → email delivery. One infrastructure build, used by all 200+ products.
LAUNCH AGENT: OpenClaw agent with multi-search-engine, cron, message, and web_fetch skills. Researches community per product, drafts Reddit/PH/HN/SEO content, schedules the launch sequence (Day 1 Reddit → Day 3 PH → Day 5 HN → Day 14 follow-up), monitors for traction, alerts via Telegram. Saves ~750 hours of manual launch work across 200 products.
PWA: Each web page is installable as a Progressive Web App on Android and iOS. Zero App Store approval. Zero 30% revenue share. Looks native on mobile. 2–4 hours of infrastructure work, applies to all products automatically.
ONE STRIPE ACCOUNT: Unlimited products. Custom statement descriptor per product. Multiple product brands under one LLC. This is standard indie hacker setup — Stripe explicitly supports it.

RISKS — HONEST
  • Launch discipline. The #1 failure mode is shipping without distributing. Each kit that launches without a Reddit post, PH listing, and SEO content is invisible. Mitigated by: Launch Agent, which doesn't skip steps.
  • SEO lag. New domains take 3–6 months to rank. The sprint launch means SEO compounding hits portfolio-wide at month 5–6 — cash flow is thin in the first months. Mitigated by: keep infrastructure costs low, Spanish/PT keywords rank faster.
  • Content farm risk. 200 thin pages on one domain could trigger a Google quality review. Mitigated by: each page is genuinely useful, state-specific, and length-appropriate — not spun content. Distribute across multiple targeted domains by niche if needed.
  • Willingness to pay variance. Some kits will convert at 2%; others at 0.1%. The portfolio approach means a few winners carry the rest. Prediction: tenant/homeowner kits convert best (immediate financial pain), musician/creator kits worst (cash-strapped demographic).
